Step 1 of 5

Couldn't save that. Check your connection and try again, or press Skip.

Skip the repeat. Keep the story.

Reprise looks across your episodes for the intros and credits that repeat, then lets you decide what is safe to skip. It never changes your media files.

Pick episodes in the Library

The Library lists your shows and episodes. Search by name, or narrow it by show and season. Tick the episodes you want checked, or open one to see what Reprise found.

Nothing listed yet? Connect your media server first, under Settings, then Connections.

Analyze them

With episodes ticked, press Analyze. Reprise compares them to find what repeats. Expect minutes to about an hour, depending on how many episodes you pick and on your hardware. It runs in the background, so you can keep browsing or close the tab.

Analyze Analyzing

Progress shows in the Activity panel. Reprise works on a CPU alone; a GPU is optional, and Settings, then Advanced lists what it found.

Review, then approve

Open an episode to see its timeline. Each section Reprise found is a suggestion: approve what is right, reject what is not, or protect a scene it must keep. Press Save review when you are done. Nothing is skipped until you approve it.

Sending to Plex stays off until you turn it on

Saving a review stays inside Reprise. Nothing goes to Plex until you switch on Allow sending to Plex under Settings, then Sharing, and every change still needs your confirmation. The shield in the sidebar always shows the current state.
