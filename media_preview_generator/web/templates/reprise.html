How Reprise decided

Reprise compares the picture and every channel of the selected audio between episodes. It does not understand the story, so a skip is only a suggestion. Scores rank suggestions; they are not probabilities. Subtitles and player overlays are not analyzed.

The comparison tolerates normal encoding noise, so tiny visual edits and quiet or brief changes can be missed. Preview every proposed skip and protect any scene you want to keep. Known colour and format details must match between files; they are never assumed.

Send to Plex

Saving a review never changes Plex. Sending results to Plex is a separate step, and it is switched off unless you turn it on under Settings, Sharing.
