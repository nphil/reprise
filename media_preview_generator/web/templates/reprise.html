The most graphics card memory Reprise can use at once. It always leaves 1 GB free for your other programs, and gives it all back when an analysis ends.

Reprise asks these public databases where an episode's intro and credits are. It sends only the show's ID, the season, the episode number and how long the file is. Reprise uses their answers as hints for where to look. Anything it suggests still needs your approval.

Sometimes Reprise can't confirm an intro or credits by comparing episodes. Then an AI model on your own network looks at a few small pictures from the episode and may suggest where it is. It only uses a model that is already loaded. It never changes what Reprise found, and nothing goes to Plex until you approve it. Off by default.

How Reprise decided

Reprise compares the picture and the sound between episodes. It doesn't understand the story, so every skip is a suggestion. A score only ranks suggestions. It isn't a percentage chance. Reprise ignores subtitles and anything the player draws on top of the picture.

Reprise ignores the small differences that come from how a file was encoded. Because of that, it can miss tiny edits and quiet or brief changes. Watch each suggested skip, and protect any scene you want to keep. It only compares files whose colour and format settings are known to match.

Send to Plex

Saving a review doesn't change Plex. Sending to Plex is a separate step, and it's off until you turn it on in Settings, Sharing.
