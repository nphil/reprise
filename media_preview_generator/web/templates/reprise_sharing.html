Phone alerts

Reprise can message your phone when suggestions are waiting for you, and when it sent something to Plex by itself. The message has buttons, so you can approve or undo straight from it without signing in.

One message per season, never one per episode, and no more than three an hour. A button only works once and stops working after six hours.
